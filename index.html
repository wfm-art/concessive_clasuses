CONCESSIVE CLAUSES

Expressing an obstacle that does not stop the action

Obstacle ≠ Stop

🚧

CONCESSIVE CLAUSE

Introduces an obstacle, difficulty, or contrasting idea.

Aunque no sepas pintar,

➡️ 🎨

MAIN CLAUSE

The action still happens despite that obstacle.

puedes venir al taller.

🚧 Aunque no sepas pintar+➡️ puedes venir al taller

The obstacle is real in the sentence, but it does not prevent the main action.
